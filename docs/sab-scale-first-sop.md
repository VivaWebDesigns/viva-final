# Local Falcon SAB Scale-First SOP v3.0

Consolidated replacement • Definitions approved for testing

This procedure replaces the previous SOP in full. When Matt designates it for a run, it is the sole procedural authority together with his explicit instructions for that run. It applies general rules only; earlier amendments, remembered workflows, research-note wording and past case rulings do not establish current policy. No new paid run is authorized by this revision. Production use requires the matching connector and CRM contract to be deployed and verified; release verification is separate from this document.

Purpose: identify relevant service-area businesses, verify a usable contact, establish credible visibility evidence and prepare a clean CRM handoff. Keep pre-outreach research focused; deeper qualification belongs after a response. Website/platform/service-page audits, review audits, routine review-recency analysis, Reviews Docs, manual Secretary of State research and sales-priority scoring are outside this workflow. Sending-domain setup, warmup, bounce handling and campaign execution are downstream controls, required before scale outreach but not performed by this collection SOP.

## 1. Ownership, authorization and testing

One Codex orchestrator owns intake, research, centering, scan decisions, reconciliation and final handoff. It may delegate bounded, read-only contact research, address-source research, checkpoint assembly and final evidence auditing; there is no separate supervisor or nested review loop. Assign nonoverlapping Place-ID groups to one worker per stage. Workers receive the exact run ID, Workflow Sheet URL and tab, assigned Place IDs, stage, permitted tools and stop condition. They cannot expand scope, change policy, authorize spending or establish authoritative workflow state. Persist verified results promptly, return accepted and rejected-candidate evidence, and have the orchestrator reconcile every worker result into authoritative state before scan planning or manifest construction.

The exact Sheet URL and tab are immutable run identifiers. Use the Viva SAB Workflow connector for company-row reads and writes; do not repoint environment variables, rely on a default Sheet or use generic Drive editing for these rows. Require a successful write receipt before claiming a save. Execution batches share one run and SOP; they do not create separate policies or audit loops.

Use the guarded connector. Initialize one run per Workflow Sheet with the orchestrator identity, authorization reference and credit ceiling. At intake, obtain and store one run-wide authorization to use verified, publicly listed business phone numbers as exact-phone Google search queries for the contact-research fallback. It applies to every company in the run and does not authorize outreach, non-public numbers or personal numbers not publicly presented as business contact information. If absent, complete the other free paths and hold only the exact-phone fallback. Record each exact batch plan before submission: company, Place ID, role, type, center and derivation, centering/corroboration evidence, grid, radius, units, keyword, platform, grid-point count, estimated credits, duplicate-report check and reason. Include any prerequisite save-location action explicitly for the exact Place ID. A roster approval does not approve an auxiliary; auxiliary approval does not approve its follow-up; one recenter approval does not approve another. Never save or alter a location, launch a campaign or change account data without exact authorization.

Preserve exact Place-ID deduplication, idempotency, spending limits and durable submission receipts. No direct paid API calls, approval resets or guessed completion. A claim stranded at `preparing_location` may continue automatically under its existing approval only when the connector verifies the exact unchanged authorization, envelope, idempotency key and credit reservation and proves the paid scan request never started through the absence of `submit_started_at`; this is continuation of the approved submission, not a retry or new charge. Record and report the submitted report key immediately. Pending or slow processing is not failure: poll within bounded limits and continue safe unaffected work instead of repeatedly waiting. A claim at `submitting`, `ambiguous_response` or any state where the paid provider request may have started remains stopped until the guarded recovery verifies the provider history and satisfies the applicable explicit-approval rule. Recover the existing claim without resetting the run. Retry only after confirmed provider failure and fresh explicit approval. Read-only work and ordinary corrections may continue within scope without repeated permission; paid enrichment remains subject to the approved run scope and cost accounting.

## 2. Discovery, qualification and focused research

Matt configures and runs the master discovery scan: practical market center, suitable tracked anchor, sufficient edge coverage and overlap only when one grid cannot reasonably cover the market. Never change the anchor business's GBP information. Matt supplies the completed report, market/state, trade, exact keyword, run authorization, credit budget, any explicit trade exceptions and supplemental duplicate sources. The assistant does not launch or replace a master scan.

Verify completion, readability, market and keyword. Record master report identity, anchor name/Place ID, center, grid, radius/unit, platform, keyword, expected coverage and actual or estimated credits when available. If identity is unresolved, ask for the correct link rather than generating another report.

Call create_sab_workflow_from_master_report once for the exact report key. Preserve the source discovery snapshot and complete ledger server-side, one row per unique Place ID; never retransmit the full competitor population through chat or create_sab_workflow. Reconcile source-business count, unique/missing/duplicate Place-ID counts, CRM duplicate counts, disposition counts, Sheet row count and Place-ID checksum, explicitly accounting for any tracked-anchor exclusion. A preservation/count mismatch stops downstream work until reconciled. Use check_crm_local_falcon_report only for a read-only diagnostic when no Sheet is being created; check_crm_place_ids is for small supplemental lists, not transferring a master roster.

Every ledger row retains identity, name, rating/reviews, categories, SAB status, available phone/website, CRM duplicate provenance, stage statuses and final disposition/reason; source grid evidence stays server-side. No lead may disappear. Set workflow to scale_first_v2 and upgrade older Sheets to the current structured schema before using new fields. Required artifacts are the discovery snapshot/ledger, connector-backed Workflow Sheet, exact-ID enrichment receipts, verified contacts and separate rejected-candidate evidence, center/authorization/scan records, one validated batch.json, import preview and funnel reconciliation. No Batch Launch Prompts Doc or Reviews Docs is required.

Deduplicate against both deliverable and CRM-only records by exact Place-ID equality before enrichment or scans; never substitute name, phone, email, website, address or fuzzy matching. An existing exact-ID match is excluded unless Matt explicitly approves reprocessing. Preserve distinct Place IDs; do not merge related listings without his ruling.

Request the applicable category, address, rating, review-count, phone and website fields in the server-side master read. The first categories entry is the GBP primary category. Confirm SAB only from address:false; any full or partial public address fails the SAB filter and is discarded, not persisted. Require at least one Google review and rating ≥4.5. Missing/nonnumeric values require resolution, not a pass. There is no other review minimum, no maximum and no standing recency gate. A narrow activity check is permitted only at Matt's request or because specific evidence creates genuine doubt; low review volume alone is insufficient. Do not routinely call the reviews endpoint.

Exclude national/multistate franchises and branded branch networks; local multi-location ownership alone is not an exclusion. Consolidate ambiguous ownership for Matt. The default trade rule requires a primary-category match. Secondary categories or broad services cannot override a clear mismatch unless Matt approves a broader run definition. Defer contradictory or ambiguous relevance; retain the exact primary category and decision evidence. Final trade relevance must be settled before paid scans.

Use enrich_sab_businesses only for survivors of inexpensive filters. Deduplicate inputs before provider requests. My Business Info Live requests use keyword place_id:<exact ID>, location_name and language_code, not a standalone place_id field. Verify exact returned-ID equality before saving. Do not use name-plus-market fallback while exact-ID lookup is available; a documented connector outage is the only fallback basis, and exact returned-ID verification still applies.

Retain compact business name, Place ID, CID, phone, website, rating/reviews, primary/additional categories and stable IDs, services summary, description, is_claimed, coordinates and place_topics when returned, plus actual cost/task receipts. Save business_profile with source=dataforseo_my_business_info_live and the matched compact business fields through the supported workflow/CRM contract; never copy full service objects or noisy raw provider payloads. Source-phone evidence does not resolve a conflicting verified contact automatically; record the verified resolution before export/outreach. A zero-cost response with no task/result is request_not_submitted, not a lookup or charge. Isolate failed/ambiguous enrichment to that company and retry or defer without repeating successful requests. Categories describe Google's taxonomy; services and descriptions describe the company's claims. Use both to assess specialist versus generalist relevance and inform concise outreach context. Profile richness and is_claimed do not prove activity; Business Info provides neither an email nor newest-review verification.

Perform focused browser research using the user's local Google results, normally about 15–40 seconds per ordinary company, longer only when needed. Search the complete GBP name verbatim, unquoted, followed by email; retain suffixes and service descriptors. Wait for the viewport and AI Overview to render, normally at least five seconds, then visually inspect the AI answer and first-page snippets. Text extraction supplements but does not replace visual inspection. If a visible result supplies sufficient verified identity context, stop; open its source only for missing evidence or a conflict.

If that search fails or is ambiguous, search the exact phone number without added words and repeat the render/visual check. This is also the primary address-discovery query. Before recording email=null, retain the exact name query in temporary evidence, confirm visual inspection, and inspect About/Contact information on any surfaced company-controlled social profile. Stop once an email is verified or these focused paths are exhausted. A browser/fetch failure is a technical limitation, not evidence that a business lacks contact information.

An email must pass one of these original verification gates: (1) its domain matches the verified company website; (2) its source contains the exact verified business phone after digits-only normalization; or (3) a free-mail address appears on a verified company-controlled website/social profile, an exact-phone source, or multiple independent sources connecting that same email to the exact company and phone. Company name plus market alone is insufficient. Multiple agreeing sources never cure a direct phone, domain or identity contradiction. Reject any candidate that fails these gates, even if it is the only result; retain rejection/reason outside the CRM manifest so it is not reconsidered. Never construct an email pattern or send a test message.

Save the accepted gate, confidence and verified source references with the contact evidence. The connector checks structured verification and contact shape; it does not independently prove source ownership or replace this research. Retain a phone returned by Local Falcon or DataForSEO and resolve conflicts before outreach. Owner name is an optional verified nonempty string or null: collect only an explicitly identified owner/founder/managing member as a byproduct, never delay the run or infer ownership from an employee, registered agent, organizer, reviewer or reply signer. Email Ready requires a verified email; Needs Email requires a verified phone and null email. Missing owner or email alone is not a blocker. Without either verified contact, do not scan or export; do not invent a Contact Blocked tag or priority score.

Contact research is incomplete until every qualified prospect has either a verified email with accepted structured evidence or a structured exhaustion record covering exact-name search, authorized exact-phone fallback and company-controlled website/social inspection. Record each path status and inspected sources, accepted evidence or rejected candidates with reasons, completion time, final exhaustion time when applicable and confirmation that no unverified email was retained. A Needs Email label alone never satisfies this gate. Stop after a verified email under the focused-research rule; mark later paths not required because verification occurred earlier. Do not build the final manifest while any qualified record lacks one of these complete outcomes.

## 3. Evidence, privacy and center definitions

Structured run state, eligibility_state, decision_state, qualification_reason and outcome are authoritative. eligibility_state records pre-scan eligibility and may authorize a paid plan; qualification_status is a separate final disposition and remains null while scan work is in progress. Record source report, evidence hash, rule IDs, selected coordinates, derivation, validation status, next action and recenter count. Notes support history only. Use ordinary write receipts during work and one critical stage-end readback of the affected structured records; repeat evidence inspection only when a conflict, missing receipt or changed state appears.

Before master centering/planning, call analyze_sab_master_centers with the exact completed report key and surviving Place IDs. Reconcile requested/found/missing counts and each missing ID, ranked-cell count/hash, exact geometry, row/column spread and cluster diagnostics. Resolve extraction failure, missing identity or checksum inconsistency before affected centering or spending. get_sab_ranked_cells is a targeted exception/verification tool, not a replacement for master reconciliation. Do not transcribe raw cells, reconstruct server results from chat, or substitute screenshot/proxy footprints. Historical five-cell and two-ring trust labels are not current policy; S01 governs bounded versus genuinely truncated evidence.

If disconnected master-scan clusters do not match an existing deterministic route, hold the company for evidence review; do not invent a center or launch a scan.

Verify completed provider status, exact report key/subject, scan specification and complete grid geometry before decisions. Use server-side extraction and compact diagnostics. Exact ranked evidence means integer ranks 1–20; ranks above 20, 20+ and unknown/unranked placeholders never enter centering, cluster, coverage or peak calculations. For saturation medians only, include every grid cell: retain exact positive ranks, and count 20+/unranked as 21, a worse-than-20 sentinel, never an observed rank. Compute the full 49-cell, outer 24-cell and central 9-cell medians separately. Keep raw ARP for policy tests and all-point ATRP for prospect-facing average position.

When the initial center remains unreliable, address corroboration is required before an auxiliary unless an approved centering rule already resolves the center. A bounded interior point source/coherent cluster does not require unnecessary address research. For an unresolved auxiliary route, complete the focused phone-anchored search and record either an evaluated fit decision or genuine no-candidate research; lack of research is not failed corroboration.

An independently discovered address is a temporary candidate, never a fact to publish. Use phone-anchored results, company-controlled contact/footer/About pages or verified directory, trade-association or licensing records. Require exact-phone, company-controlled publication or independent consistent identity evidence. Residential, suite or mail-receiving type alone is not grounds for rejection: the defensible center is the point Google associates with the listing, not an assumed operating yard.

For each candidate, use record_sab_address_corroboration, which calls evaluate_sab_address_candidate with the exact completed report key/Place ID and ephemeral candidate. Apply the complete-distribution fit decision using the centroid, spread, nearest pin, strongest cluster and truthful geocoder precision. Retain the approximately three-mile fit limit and absence of shape contradiction; proximity to one stray pin is insufficient. The evaluator measures evidence; it does not determine business identity or make the final fit judgment. Record source type, identity method, fit rationale/distances, report/hash and accepted coordinates in decision_state.address_corroboration. An accepted fit can establish corroborated_address regardless of ranked-cell count. If an already reliable centroid conflicts with an address, reject the stale address and retain the centroid; do not buy an auxiliary solely because of that conflict.

Unavailable evaluation, partial geocoding, missing exact Place ID or irreconcilable evidence holds only the affected company. These are incomplete/technical-failure outcomes, never rejected-address/no-candidate evidence and never automatic permission for an auxiliary. Resolve the failure before advancing that route. Retry a technical writer failure only through `record_sab_address_corroboration`; it must freshly verify the same current report and evidence before replacing the hold with an accepted/rejected candidate or completed no-candidate result. A partial known candidate remains incomplete and cannot be relabelled as no candidate. A completed rejected fit or completed no-candidate search may support the applicable S01 auxiliary plan.

Hidden street addresses must not enter the ledger, Sheets, manifests, CRM, notes, chat, handoffs, previews, reports or outreach. Use a raw candidate only within active corroboration; persist safe fit metadata and coordinates, never the address or raw grid. A worker that discovers a candidate must complete its fit evaluation itself; if it cannot, reserve address discovery to the orchestrator rather than collecting/transferring the raw address. Correct any privacy breach before export or handoff. Every CRM/export address is exactly Service Area Business; never replace it with a research address or market location.

Adjacent cells share an edge or corner (eight neighbors). A coherent cluster contains at least two exact top-20 cells connected under that adjacency rule. A dominant peak is at least three ranks better than the median exact pin. Form best+2 contiguous clusters containing a globally best pin. Choose greatest total 1/rank weight, then nearest cluster centroid to the prior center, then northernmost and westernmost cell. Among tied best pins, choose nearest prior center, then row and column.

When the current central 3×3 contains a coherent cluster, an off-center peak may trigger recentering only when all three tests pass: its selected best pin is at least three ranks better than the best central pin; the 3×3 centered on the selected peak pin has a better median than the current central 3×3; and the candidate 3×3 contains at least as many exact top-20 pins as the current central 3×3. For these neighborhood medians only, assign 20+ and unranked cells the computational sentinel 21; never persist 21 as an observed rank. If any test fails, retain the existing center as unsupported_off_center_peak unless S05 independently requires a different route. If the selected peak's 3×3 extends beyond the scan grid, do not infer missing ranks; apply the existing higher-priority boundary rule. This safeguard does not apply when the current central 3×3 has no exact top-20 visibility or no coherent cluster; use the existing point-source and boundary routes.

Peak targeting applies to the first post-auxiliary deliverable and every recenter. Use the selected peak cluster’s 1/rank centroid when dominant. Otherwise a whole-field centroid is allowed only if it does not move away from the selected best pin and remains within one diagonal cell of the chosen cluster on the proposed 7×7/3-mile grid (√2 miles). If either check fails, use the selected best pin. A recenter must produce verified movement toward that peak; never submit the unchanged center as a recenter.

## 4. Scan decision table

Precedence: authorization/privacy, valid evidence and required corroboration first; apply the current stage only. On auxiliaries, exclusion precedes visibility disposition. On three-mile deliverables/recenters, assess saturation before margin/recentering; a supported off-center peak fails centering under S04/S07. A five-mile comparison preserves the already validated three-mile center: assess S09 and, absent an approved exclusion, apply S08 without another centering test. S09 never validates a center. Proposed exclusions pause for Matt before any final disposition. Testing approval always gates the next paid batch. Standard deliverable: Google 7×7/3 miles, exact run keyword. Routine auxiliaries: scout 9×9/6 miles or justified fine scan 7×7/1.5 miles. Permit one auxiliary and one routine recenter per lead; additional attempts need an explicit exception. Permit at most one five-mile comparison per lead.

## 6. Stage handoff and approval

A handoff contains the exact run/Sheet/tab, assigned Place IDs, structured stage and next action, source report references, receipt/evidence hashes, verified/rejected contact evidence, safe corroboration status, credit commitment, current testing approval/stop, any explicit exception and remaining blockers. Do not reopen verified sources routinely or paste raw grids/hidden addresses. Distinguish verified facts from inherited conclusions and agent error from a proposed policy correction.

Continue in the current task while it can operate safely. A large payload, long checkpoint or guessed context limit does not justify a replacement task. If an actual mechanical limit requires continuation, the new orchestrator reads this complete current SOP and resumes from verified connector state. A handoff transfers identifiers and state, not new policy; completion means the run objective is finished, not merely that a handoff is ready.

Before contact research: verify master identity/specification, complete discovery/unique-ID preservation, exact CRM deduplication and inexpensive filter evidence/counts.
