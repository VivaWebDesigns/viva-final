The starting point

A Good Company That Google Was Barely Showing

Glass and Door Pro already had the experience, services and customer reputation to compete. The problem was visibility.

Its previous website was poorly built for local SEO, failed to clearly represent the quality of the company's work and attracted searches for auto glass instead of the residential and commercial glass services it actually provided.

Even when customers searched close to the company's Monroe location, Google frequently showed competitors first.

Before Viva
  • Barely visible on Google Maps
  • Ranking for the wrong types of searches
  • Receiving only 2–3 calls per week

The ranking data

The Same Business. The Same Market. Sixty Days Apart.

Each scan measures where the business appears when customers search from different points throughout the market. Lower ranking numbers indicate stronger placement in Google's local results.

Before

Barely Visible Within 1.5 Miles

The business did not appear across the wider Charlotte-area scans. The scan radius had to be reduced to only 1.5 miles and centered directly on the company before meaningful ranking data appeared.

Takeaway: Customers could be located only a few miles away and still be shown competitors first.

60 Days Later
After 60 days

Ranked Number One Across the Original Radius

Using the same 1.5-mile grid, the business reached the number-one position across the measured area. The results were strong enough to justify expanding the scan.

Takeaway: The company was no longer merely appearing—it was leading the local results.

Expanded scan

Strong Rankings Across a 12-Mile Market

The scan radius was increased eightfold to test how far the improved visibility extended. The company continued ranking strongly throughout Monroe and nearby areas, with visibility beginning to push toward Charlotte.

Takeaway: The improvement held well beyond the company's immediate location.

About these results

Results vary based on market size, competition, business history and the condition of the existing website and Google Business Profile. These results reflect one completed client project and are not a guarantee of identical outcomes.

The starting point

A Strong Service That Google Was Barely Surfacing

Carolina Custom Automation installs smart-home and automation systems, but its original online presence was not producing regular calls or emails from Google.

The first 3-mile scan was taken around the company's former rural Clover location. Visibility was inconsistent, with rankings weakening sharply across much of the measured area.

After the new website and Google Business Profile work, the company moved to Fort Mill—a more populated market with more nearby competitors.

Before Viva
  • Inconsistent visibility across the original 3-mile scan
  • Competing from a rural market with limited reach
  • No regular calls or emails coming from Google

The ranking data

A Tougher Market. A Much Stronger Result.

The business changed locations between the first and second scans, so this is not a same-address comparison. The later scans show how Carolina Custom Automation performed after entering the denser Fort Mill market.

Original Clover scan

Limited Visibility Across the Original 3 Miles

The original scan around the rural Clover location showed a small pocket of strength, but rankings weakened quickly across much of the area. Many points ranked between 7 and 20+, limiting how often nearby customers would see the company first.

Takeaway: The business had little dependable reach and was not receiving regular Google inquiries.

60 Days Later + Fort Mill Move
60 days later

Dominant Rankings Across the Fort Mill Core

After the website and Google Business Profile work, the company moved into Fort Mill. Despite the larger population and more competitive business environment, the 3-mile scan showed number-one rankings across nearly the entire measured area.

Takeaway: Visibility improved dramatically even though the company entered a tougher market.

Newest 5-mile scan

Strong Visibility Across a Larger Market

The newest scan expanded the radius from 3 to 5 miles. Carolina Custom Automation continued ranking at or near the top across Fort Mill and surrounding areas, with expected competition increasing toward the outer northern edge.

Takeaway: The strong core rankings held as the measured market expanded.
