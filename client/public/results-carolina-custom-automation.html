The starting point

A strong business.
Room to be found.

Carolina Custom Automation’s original online presence was not bringing in regular calls or emails from Google. Its first scan, around rural Clover, showed inconsistent visibility. After the website and profile work, the business moved to Fort Mill, where the population and competitive landscape were different.

What we changed

  1. Built a clearer website

    Explained the company’s automation services and gave prospective customers a direct way to call or email.

  2. Organized the services

    Structured the website around the work customers search for, helping Google understand the company’s relevance.

  3. Aligned and measured the presence

    Optimized the Google Business Profile, repeated the scan after the move, and expanded the measured area from 36 to 100 square miles.

About this comparison

The business moved from rural Clover to Fort Mill between the original and later scans. Location and competitor density changed, so this is not a controlled, same-location comparison. The maps document visibility before and after the move.

These are historical project results. Outcomes vary by market, competition, business history, and starting condition. Call and lead figures are client-reported; ranking maps are not call-tracking reports.
