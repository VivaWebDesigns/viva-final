Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

Viva Web Designs LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and business name when you contact us or request a visibility scan.
  • Usage Data: Device and browser information, pages viewed, approximate location, referring pages, interactions, and visit times collected automatically through cookies and analytics tools.
  • Communications: Messages you send us through contact forms, email, or SMS.
  • Business Information: Your trade, primary service, city or target market, business address or Google Business Profile link, and other details you provide for an inquiry or visibility scan.
  • SMS Consent Information: Whether you opted in to text messages, the form used, the date and time of consent, and the consent disclosure presented to you.
  • Report Engagement Data: When we email you a personalized visibility report, we may record when the secure report link is used and when a report call-to-action is selected. Automated security scanners may also access email links, so a recorded access does not always establish that the intended recipient personally viewed the report.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Prepare and deliver requested visibility scans, proposals, and services.
  • Contact you about your inquiry, requested services, appointments, project updates, or customer support.
  • If you separately opt in, send text messages about your inquiry, requested services, appointments, and related updates. SMS consent is not a condition of purchase.
  • Improve our website, services, and marketing materials.
  • Measure engagement with visibility reports and follow up on actions taken through those reports.
  • Comply with legal obligations.
  • Send email communications you have opted into.

3. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for those third parties’ own marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us operate our website and business, such as scheduling, payment, hosting, or support providers. We require or expect these service providers to handle personal information only as necessary to perform services on our behalf and in accordance with applicable law.

We may also disclose information if required by law or if necessary to protect our business, clients, or website.

4. Mobile Information and SMS Consent

Providing a phone number or submitting a form does not by itself enroll you in text messages. If you check the optional SMS consent box, we may text you about your inquiry, requested services, appointments, and related updates. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

No Mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP. Message and data rates may apply.

Message frequency varies. Reply HELP for help.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics, deployed through Google Tag Manager on our main public pages and through a Google tag on personalized report pages, to measure website traffic and understand how visitors interact with our pages, forms, and report calls-to-action. We do not send names, email addresses, CRM lead identifiers, or secure report-delivery tokens to Google Analytics. Google may collect or receive device, browser, interaction, approximate location, and cookie or similar identifier data and process it under Google’s policies. Google Analytics may process IP addresses during collection to derive location information, but Google states that GA4 discards IP addresses before logging them.

You can instruct your browser to refuse or delete cookies. You can also learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services and use Google’s available browser opt-out tools. Some website features may not function properly without cookies.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide requested services, maintain business and consent records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal, accounting, or reporting obligations. Retention periods vary based on the type of information and why it was collected. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

9. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take steps to delete it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold about you and to opt out of marketing communications. Contact us at matt@vivawebdesigns.com to exercise these rights.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will indicate significant changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
