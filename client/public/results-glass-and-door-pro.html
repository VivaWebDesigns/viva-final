The starting point

A strong business.
Room to be found.

Glass and Door Pro had the experience and reputation to compete, but its website wasn’t clearly representing its residential and commercial services. It attracted searches for auto glass, and competitors often appeared first—even close to the company’s Monroe location.

What we changed

  1. Rebuilt the website

    Created a custom website around the residential and commercial glass services the company actually wanted to sell.

  2. Corrected the search foundations

    Improved service targeting, page structure, and the signals that help Google understand the business.

  3. Strengthened the Google profile

    Aligned the Google Business Profile with the company’s services, location, and target market.
